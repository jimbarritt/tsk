# Mission: Idea capture

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| ID | M-ADMIN-01 |
| Territory | agentic research |
| Assignee | Jim, with a session agent recording |
| Blocked by | none |

## Objective

- An idea Jim states in a session is written to the ledger before that session ends.
- The written idea uses Jim's own terminology, with no label or shorthand invented for
  it.
- The ledger's record of an idea says who raised it and when.

## Intelligence

- [../../future-missions-tbd.md](../../future-missions-tbd.md): where a captured idea
  goes, and the format existing entries use.
- `docs/domain/mission-model.md` in the tsk repo, under mission categories: why this is a
  mission rather than a new kind of object, and where tsk
  deviates from doctrine in saying so.
- `docs/domain/ubiquitous-language.md` in the tsk repo, Objective: the two objective

## Decision authority

Yours: where an idea is written, and the wording that records it.

Not yours: whether an idea is worth doing, what it is called, its ordering against
anything else, and whether it becomes a mission. Jim decides all four. Recording an idea
commits to nothing.

## Constraints

- Capture only. Do not expand on an idea unless Jim asks.
- Use Jim's terminology. If a concept needs a name, Jim names it, or asks for options.
- One idea per entry, so a later decision can act on it alone.

## Out of scope

- Shaping a captured idea into a mission briefing.
- Judging an idea, or ranking it against another.
- Executing any work the idea describes.
- Rewriting an earlier entry to match a later one.

## First behaviour

Say you are ready to capture, and wait. Do not open the conversation by proposing ideas.
