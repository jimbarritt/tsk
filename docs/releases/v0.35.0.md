Remove commands for records (M-TSK-ADHOC T-24). The ledger is still at layout 10.

- **`tsk task rm <mission> <T-nn> [--force]`.** Removes a task. It refuses while another task is blocked by it or blocks it, or a decision names it, and says what it needs. `--force` drops the blocks and clears the task of the decisions.
- **`tsk decision rm <mission> <n>`.** Removes the decision at place `n` of `tsk decision list <mission>`.
- **`tsk question rm <mission> <Q-nn>`.** Removes a question, open or answered.
- **`tsk mission rm <id> [--force]`.** Always refuses a mission that has child missions. It refuses a mission that holds tasks, decisions or questions, has threads, blocks or blockers, or links documents, unless `--force`. `--force` removes the tasks, decisions and questions with the mission, drops its blocks in both directions, unlinks its documents and detaches its threads from it. The documents and the threads stay. Nothing changes when the command refuses. It prints one line for each change.
- A removal is a row delete in a local Dolt commit, so the history of the ledger keeps what was there. `tsk ledger push` publishes it.
- Store: `LedgerStore::mission_remove` deletes the mission row and every row that names it in one write.
- Tests: `cli/src/mission/rm.rs` (the plans) and `cli/tests/conformance.rs` (refusals, `--force`, a push and a fetch on a second clone).
- The plugin requires tsk 0.35.0 or later.
