The agent guide says how to read a mission.

- `tsk guide import` now opens with a section that says the records are the source of truth for missions, tasks, decisions and questions, that documents hold free text only, and that the same fact is not written in both places.
- The guide says how to read a mission in full: `tsk mission list <id> --json` prints the purpose, constraints, objectives, blocks, linked documents, tasks, decisions and questions, open and answered.
- The guide says that projections, which render the records as Markdown for people, are planned and not built. Until they are, an agent reads the JSON and writes the summary or table itself.
- The rest of the guide still describes the document form of a plan. It is being rewritten for the record commands, and the new section wins where the two disagree.
